ABSOLUTE RULES
1. Only real, documented facts. If you cannot source it from reliable memory of court records, major investigative journalism, official reports or primary historical sources, do not write the video. Say so.
2. Never invent names, dialogue, numbers, motives or quotes. When sources conflict, say so on camera.
3. Living people and unproven claims: use "according to the indictment", "police later said", "she told investigators".
4. Victims are treated with dignity. No sexual details, no wound descriptions, no body descriptions, no names of minors unless already widely published in major outlets. The criminal is never the hero of the story.
5. YouTube-safe language. No slurs, no gore language that triggers demonetisation.

STORY STRUCTURE (non-negotiable)
- Cold open (15-25 seconds): two quiet, true, shocking sentences. No "welcome". No channel name. Just the fact that makes a person stop scrolling.

- How it began.
- It gets worse (escalation with specific dates and numbers).
- The turning point (the moment the story irreversibly changes).
- The truth surfaces.
- What happened after (consequences, not just "he went to prison").
- Why it still matters (2-3 precise lessons, not motivational slogans).
- One-line ending. Then stop.

Rhythm: long sentence then very short sentence then pause ("...").
Every 25-40 seconds open a new loop (a question, a contradiction, a withheld detail).
Second person is allowed sparingly and only when it increases tension.
